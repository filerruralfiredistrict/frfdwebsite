Transparency

Filer Rural Fire District is committed to open and accountable governance.

Annual Budget

The district budget is approved by the Board of Fire Commissioners each September at the start of the new fiscal year. Budget documents are posted to Google Drive following board approval.

  • 📁 Budget & Financial Documents (Google Drive)

Meeting Agendas

Board meeting agendas are posted at least 2 days prior to each meeting. See the Governance page for links to all agendas by year.

Meeting Minutes

Meeting minutes are available upon request. Contact the district office to request copies. Minutes for some past meetings may also be available within the meeting agenda folders on Google Drive.

Document Templates

The following templates are provided for district use when preparing meeting agendas and minutes.

  • 📄 Board Meeting Agenda Template (.docx)
  • 📄 Board Meeting Minutes Template (.docx)

State Financial Transparency (Transparent Idaho)

Idaho law (Idaho Code 67-1075/67-1076) requires local governmental entities to report financial data to the state. Filer Rural Fire District's budget, revenues, and expenditure information is available on the Idaho State Controller's Transparent Idaho portal.

To view our data: visit the link below, select Fire Protection District as the entity type, and search for Filer Rural Fire District.

  • 📈 Search Transparent Idaho — Local Districts

Budget Hearing Notice

Idaho Code 31-1422 requires the district to provide public notice of budget hearings at least 10 days in advance. Budget hearing notices are published in a local newspaper of general circulation and posted at the district station (100 US-30, Filer, ID 83328). Upcoming budget hearing details will also be posted on the Board Meetings page and announced at regular board meetings.

Public Records Requests

Under the Idaho Public Records Act (Idaho Code Title 74, Chapter 1), residents have the right to request district records. To submit a public records request, contact us at:

  • Phone: (208) 326-4111
  • Address: 100 US-30, Filer, ID 83328
  • Online: Contact form — select "Public Records Request" in the subject field

The district will respond to records requests as required by Idaho law.
