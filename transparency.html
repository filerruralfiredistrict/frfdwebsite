Transparency

Filer Rural Fire District is committed to open and accountable governance.

Annual Budget

The district budget is approved by the Board of Fire Commissioners each September at the start of the new fiscal year. Budget documents are posted to Google Drive following board approval.

  • 📁 Budget & Financial Documents (Google Drive)

Meeting Agendas

Board meeting agendas are posted at least 2 days prior to each meeting. See the Governance page for links to all agendas by year.

Meeting Minutes

Meeting minutes are available upon request. Contact the district office to request copies. Minutes for some past meetings may also be available within the meeting agenda folders on Google Drive.
