Board Meetings

The Filer Rural Fire District Board of Directors meets regularly to conduct district business. Meetings are open to the public.

Board meetings are held on the second Monday of each month at 7:00 PM in the Commissioner's Room at Station 26, 100 US-30, Filer, ID 83328. Dates and times subject to change — call (208) 326-4111 to confirm.

Meeting Agendas

Agendas are posted to Google Drive at least 2 days before each meeting. Click a year to view and download agendas — no Google account required.

Public Comment

Members of the public are welcome to attend board meetings and may address the board during the public comment period. Please contact the district in advance if you plan to attend.
