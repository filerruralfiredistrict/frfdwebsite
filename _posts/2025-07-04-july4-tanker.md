Filer Rural Fire District is proud to announce that our newest fire engine is now in service. The new apparatus — Engine #4 — joins our fleet and strengthens our ability to respond to fire emergencies throughout the district.

The district maintains an ISO Class 3 rating, reflecting our commitment to high-quality fire protection for the community.

With dry summer conditions and elevated fire risk, we remind all residents to use fireworks responsibly and follow all local ordinances. If you see a fire, call 911 immediately.
