Filer Rural Fire District coordinates with Air St. Luke's to provide air medical transport for critical patients in our coverage area. When ground transport time is a factor, our crews work directly with the Air St. Luke's flight team to ensure patients receive the fastest possible care.

This partnership is part of our broader mutual aid network and reflects our commitment to providing the highest level of emergency services to the Filer community.
