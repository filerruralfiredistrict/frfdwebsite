Filer Rural Fire District is actively recruiting volunteer firefighters to serve our community. We currently operate with one full-time staff member and approximately 30 paid on-call volunteers — and we need more dedicated people to join our team.

## Why Volunteer?

- **Make a difference** — play a crucial role in protecting the safety and well-being of your neighbors
- **Full training provided** — no experience necessary, we'll train you from the ground up
- **Build community connections** — work alongside dedicated volunteers who share your commitment to the community
- **Flexible scheduling** — paid on-call structure works around your existing commitments

## How to Apply

Stop by the station at **100 US-30, Filer, ID 83328** during business hours (Monday–Friday, 8am–5pm), or call us at **(208) 326-4111**. You can also submit an interest form on our [volunteer page](/volunteer.html).

We'd love to have you on the team.
